Chapter 3 supplementary videos
This folder contains supplementary videos associated with Chapter 2 of the thesis.
The videos illustrate different aspects of the cell migration model, including the role of active force generation, adhesion dynamics and temporal coordination between polymerisation and contraction forces.

Videos
	•	Video_S3_1_Cell_on_rigid_substrate_Logistic_profile.mp4: Cell migrating on a rigid substrate. The force profiles follow a logistic-type functional form. 
	•	Video_S3_2_Cell_on_fibres_Jump_profile.mp4: Cell migration model on an elastic fibrous medium. The force profiles follow a jump-type functional form. 
	•	Video_S3_3_Cell_on_fibres_Triangular_profile.mp4: Cell migration model on an elastic fibrous medium. The force profiles follow a triangular-type functional form. 
	•	Video_S3_4_Cell_on_fibres_Logistic_profile.mp4: Cell migration model on an elastic fibrous medium. The force profiles follow a logistic-type functional form. 
	•	Video_S3_5_Cell_on_viscoelastic_substrate.mp4: Cell migration model on an isotropic viscoelastic continuous medium. The force profiles follow a logistic-type functional form. 
	•	Video_S3_6_Cell_on_horizontally_pre-stretched_fibrous_substrate.mp4: Cell migrating on a horizontally stretched fibrous substrate. The force profiles follow a logistic-type functional form. 
	•	Video_S3_7_Cell_on_vertically_pre-stretched_fibrous_substrate.mp4: Cell migrating on a vertically stretched fibrous substrate. The force profiles follow a logistic-type functional form.

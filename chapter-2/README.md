# Chapter 2 supplementary videos

This folder contains supplementary videos associated with Chapter 2 of the thesis. The videos illustrate different aspects of the cell--ECM interaction model, with particular emphasis on active contraction, FA dynamics and mechanically induced cell detachment.

## Videos

* [Video S2.1](Video_S2_1_PhasePortrait_TransversalFibres.mp4): **Cell detachment from the ECM.**
  Simulation illustrating how cell-generated traction forces acting on the FAs can induce detachment from a stiff ECM. The ECM configuration is such that the pulling force predominantly stretches the integrins rather than deforming the ECM fibres. The rapid increase in integrin extension drives the FA dynamics into the basin of attraction associated with detachment.

* [Video S2.2](Video_S2_2_PhasePortrait_Detachment.mp4): **Entry into the basin of attraction associated with detachment.**
  Simulation in which two elongated cells detach from a stiff ECM. The traction forces exerted by the cells stretch the integrins more rapidly than the ECM fibres deform. Consequently, the FA dynamics enter the basin of attraction associated with detachment.

* [Video S2.3](Video_S2_3_PhasePortrait_NoDetachment.mp4): **Phase-plane evolution of cells that remain attached to the ECM.**
  Simulation in which two elongated cells deform a stiff ECM without detaching. Although the pulling forces stretch the integrins, part of the mechanical energy is dissipated through deformation of the ECM fibres. The FA dynamics therefore remain outside the basin of attraction associated with detachment, and the cells remain attached.

* [Video S2.4](Video_S2_4_PhasePortrait_Detachment.mp4): **Phase-plane evolution of cells that detach from the ECM.**
  Detailed evolution of the phase-plane trajectory for the detachment case shown in Fig. A.2. The traction forces acting on the FAs drive the system into the basin of attraction associated with detachment, ultimately leading to cell release from the ECM.
